A Personal Reflection

Looking back on my journey, I am grateful for the experiences, challenges, and opportunities that have shaped both my career and my outlook on the future.

Photo by Ivana Cajina at Unsplash

The Big Picture

Miller’s Professional Imaging and Mpix are the two brands under the Miller’s, Inc umbrella. Both of which are sites where you can upload your pictures and turn them into numerous different products; from professionally framed photographic prints to greeting cards, custom drinkware, and calendars. The Miller’s Professional Imaging brand is geared toward professional photography studios, while Mpix products are marketed directly to consumers. From my very first day it was clear to me that this company prioritized one thing above all others: producing a quality product in an efficient, cost-effective way (“If the customer’s order is incorrect, then it doesn’t really matter how quickly it got done” remains a common mantra you will hear at every stage of production).

Lessons Learned

Over the years, I have come to understand just how interconnected every part of the company is. My experience working in production and later managing inventory has given me a perspective that I believe is valuable in my current role as Purchasing Manager. The decisions I make regarding purchasing and inventory can directly impact the people working on the production floor and, ultimately, the experience of the customer. Having worked my way through several different positions within the company has helped me better understand the challenges faced by different departments and the importance of communication and cooperation between them.

The Road So Far

When I first accepted a seasonal position with Miller's Professional Imaging, I never imagined that it would eventually become a career that has now spanned more than 15 years. I certainly did not begin with a detailed plan of eventually becoming a Purchasing Manager. Instead, each opportunity and promotion allowed me to develop new skills and take on greater responsibilities. Looking back, I have learned that career growth does not always happen according to a carefully planned path. Sometimes, it comes from being willing to learn, accepting new challenges, and making the most of the opportunities that are presented to you.

Looking Ahead

My career experience has also played a significant role in my decision to continue my education. After spending many years learning through hands-on experience, I realized that I wanted to continue expanding my knowledge and developing new skills. Returning to school while working full time and raising a family has been challenging, but it has also been rewarding. As I continue my education, my goal is to build upon the experience I have gained throughout my career and prepare myself for whatever opportunities and challenges may come next.
