About Me

My name is Adam Hemmann. I was born in Columbia, Missouri, and still live there with my family. For the past 15 years I have worked for an online printing company called Mpix where I currently serve as the purchasing and inventory manager. Working full time, raising a family, and being enrolled part time in school does not leave a ton of free time. That is not a complaint, though; I amfortunate to have such a full life. When I do have some free time, I usually like to spend it reading, going to my son's baseball games, or running. My wife and I are currently training for a half marathon in November. I am proud of the fact that I am able to balance a career, family, and continuing my education while working toward my long-term educational and professional goals.

Career Overview

I began my career at Miller's Professional Imaging in Columbia, Missouri as a seasonal production team member in August 2011, working in the Print Finishing Department. If I'm being honest, I initially took the job primarily because it offered health insurance, and I was about to lose coverage under my parents' plan after turning 26. What began as a practical decision, however, turned into a long-term career with opportunities for growth and advancement.

After nearly four years with the company, I received my first promotion to Finish Department Lead in July 2015. Serving as a department lead opened my eyes to what it means to manage and lead a team while also giving me my first experience managing product inventory. In February 2020, I was promoted to Warehouse Manager, a role that significantly expanded my responsibilities from managing inventory for a single department to overseeing product inventory across the company. Two years later, in February 2022, I was promoted to my current role as Purchasing Manager.
