My Career Journey

Each role has helped me build the skills and experience that shape who I am today.

Photo by v2osk at Unsplash

February 2022 - Present

Miller's Professional Imaging/Mpix, Purchasing Manager

  • Manage purchasing, inventory, and vendor relationships
  • Ensure product availability and cost-efficiency
  • Support company goals through strategic planning and process improvement

After nearly two years in the Warehouse Manager role, our Columbia production facility finally reached a size that required its own dedicated purchasing manager. Previously, there had been only one purchasing manager at our Pittsburg, KS location who was responsible for both the Columbia and Pittsburg inventories. In this role, I have helped facilitate the transition from an old and outdated accounting and inventory software to the much more modern Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central. This transition required numerous positions and departments working in concert to ensure that everything went smoothly.

February 2020 - February 2022

Miller's Professional Imaging/Mpix, Warehouse Manager

  • Managed product inventory across all areas of production
  • Coordinated with production and shipping teams
  • Led reorganization and relocation initiative during the COVID-19 pandemic

My second promotion was to the position of Warehouse Manager. In response to challenges presented by the COVID-19 pandemic, the company decided to reorganize which products were to be produced in Columbia and which were to be produced at our sister-site in Pittsburg, Kansas. One of my biggest achievements occurred during this time when I was required to assist in the move of over 300 pallets out of our warehouse in Columbia to the company’s other production facility in Pittsburg, Kansas.

July 2015 - February 2020

Miller's Professional Imaging, Finish Department Team Lead

  • Led a team and established best practices
  • Trained new and seasonal team members
  • Gained first-hand experience managing product inventory

This was my first promotion within the company, as well as my first job ever in which I was leading a team of people. I was responsible for establishing best practices across various areas of production. It was also my responsibility to train other new, seasonal team members during our seasonal hiring time each fall.

August 2011 - July 2015

Miller's Professional Imaging, Finish Department Team Member

  • Worked in the Print Finishing Department
  • Cross-trained in other areas of production
  • Focused on teamwork and producing a high-quality end product

My first role with the company began with me taking a seasonal position. Teamwork, communication, and producing high-quality work were emphasized. I was familiarized with various production processes of our department, as well as cross-trained in other departments elsewhere in production.
